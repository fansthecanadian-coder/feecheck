How to price a handmade product

A handmade item costs more than its raw materials. FeeCheck treats your production time as a cost by multiplying the time needed for one item by the hourly pay you enter. It then adds materials, packaging and overhead before accounting for selling fees and your target profit margin.

Why include labor?

Leaving your time out can make an item appear profitable even when the sale does not compensate you for making it. This calculator keeps labor compensation separate from profit so you can see both.

What does the target price mean?

The target price is the estimated selling price required for the inputs you supplied to be covered while leaving your chosen profit margin. It is a planning number, not a prediction of what customers will pay.
