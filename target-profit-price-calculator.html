Reverse profit calculation

A normal profit calculator starts with a selling price and tells you what is left. This calculator reverses the question: you choose the profit you want, and it solves for the selling price required after costs and fees.

Formula: (item cost + shipping + desired profit + fixed fee) ÷ (1 − percentage fee rate).

Useful before listing or accepting an offer

If you know the minimum profit that makes a sale worthwhile, this gives you a price floor. You can compare that result with realistic market prices before deciding whether to list, discount or accept an offer.
