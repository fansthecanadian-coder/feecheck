How it works

Your break-even price is the point where the money left after percentage and fixed marketplace fees exactly covers your item and shipping costs. Below this price, the sale loses money; above it, the difference becomes profit.

Formula: (item cost + shipping cost + fixed fee) ÷ (1 − fee rate).

Why calculate break-even before listing?

Percentage fees rise with the sale price, so simply adding the fee percentage to your costs gives the wrong answer. FeeCheck solves for the sale price itself, giving you a practical floor before you list or accept an offer.
