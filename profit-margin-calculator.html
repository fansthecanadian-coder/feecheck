Profit margin vs. markup

Profit margin measures profit as a percentage of the selling price. It is different from markup, which compares the increase in price with your cost. For sellers, margin is useful because it shows how much of each sales dollar remains after the costs entered here.

Include the costs that change the decision

Use the actual item cost, shipping you pay, and the fee settings that apply to your sale. Marketplace fee structures vary, so check your platform's current fee schedule rather than relying on an old remembered percentage.
