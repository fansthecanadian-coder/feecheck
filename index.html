Know what you’ll make — or what you need to charge.
FeeCheck uses the fee rate you enter, so it works with any marketplace. Fees vary by platform, category, region and seller status; verify the marketplace’s current fee schedule before relying on an estimate.

Reverse marketplace fee calculator

Most fee calculators answer “what will I make if I sell for this price?” FeeCheck can also work backward. Enter the profit you want, your item and shipping costs, and the marketplace fee. It calculates the minimum listing price needed to reach that target under those assumptions.

This is useful when deciding whether an item is worth listing before you spend time photographing, packing or posting it.
